# A tour of any village variant, for the parity cases tour_* (phase 1
# rollout, tools/parity/cases.txt): fade-in, a settled shot, then a fixed
# walk in all four directions, long enough to scroll the largest village
# (2820x1442; the player moves 200 px/s), the menu (its thumbnail is a copy of
# the village frame) and back. No route per place: the walk bumps into
# obstacles and villagers, which is fine in a deterministic run.
#
# The directions are relative to the start facing ("heading"), which points
# into the place: a start at the edge of a town (where the world map enters)
# or above the exit of an interior. "guard 24" lets go of a direction 24 px
# before the player would leave the place (its edge or a hole door), so the
# tour stays in it: an interior entered with --start has no town to go back
# to (its exit door would load the town at start -1, with no player). The
# "pos" lines print the player's position, facing and the background scroll.
#
# Fade-in.
wait 100
shot t01_fadein
wait 250
shot t02_fadein_later
wait 1150
shot t03_settled
pos start
heading
guard 24
log start
# Ahead for 3 s.
press AHEAD
wait 1500
shot t04_ahead
wait 1500
release AHEAD
wait 100
shot t05_ahead_end
pos ahead
# To the left for 4 s.
press ALEFT
wait 2000
shot t06_left
wait 2000
release ALEFT
wait 100
shot t07_left_end
pos left
# To the right for 8 s.
press ARIGHT
wait 2700
shot t08_right
wait 2700
shot t09_right_later
wait 2600
release ARIGHT
wait 100
shot t10_right_end
pos right
log before_menu
# The menu and back.
tap TRIANGLE
wait 2500
shot t11_menu
tap CIRCLE
wait 2000
shot t12_after_menu
log after_menu
# Back (towards the start side) for 4 s.
press BEHIND
wait 2000
shot t13_behind
wait 2000
release BEHIND
wait 100
shot t14_behind_end
pos behind
wait 1500
shot t15_end
pos end
log end
quit
