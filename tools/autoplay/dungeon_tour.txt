# A tour of a dungeon floor, one script for every floor (parity cases
# dungeon_tour_*): --load=<save> --start="<Area>--<Floor>-mark0"
# --no-encounters (no random battles; battles have cases of their own).
# No route per floor: the fade-in, a settled shot, then a fixed sequence of
# steps (UP, DOWN backwards) and turns both ways, with shots in the middle of
# a step and of a turn (the camera interpolation) and pos lines; a step
# into a wall does nothing. It ends with the minimap visible and the menu
# (its thumbnail of the floor), and back.
wait 100
shot d01_fadein
wait 400
shot d02_fadein_later
wait 2500
shot d03_settled
pos start
log start
tap UP
wait 250
shot d04_step_mid
wait 450
pos step1
tap UP
wait 700
pos step2
shot d05_ahead
tap LEFT
wait 250
shot d06_turn_left_mid
wait 450
pos left
tap UP
wait 700
tap UP
wait 700
pos left_steps
shot d07_left
tap RIGHT
wait 700
tap RIGHT
wait 250
shot d08_turn_right_mid
wait 450
pos turned_back
tap UP
wait 700
tap UP
wait 700
tap UP
wait 700
pos back_steps
shot d09_back
tap RIGHT
wait 700
tap UP
wait 700
tap UP
wait 700
pos right_steps
shot d10_right
tap DOWN
wait 350
shot d11_backwards_mid
wait 350
pos backwards
tap LEFT
wait 700
tap UP
wait 700
pos last
wait 1000
shot d12_minimap
log minimap
tap TRIANGLE
wait 2500
shot d13_menu
log menu
tap CIRCLE
wait 2000
shot d14_back
log back
quit
