# Opens a riddle, shows it and leaves it with CROSS (parity cases
# dungeon_riddle_*, phase 2 rollout; the riddles of src/miniGames: RiddleGame, GriveraRiddle and AegisRiddle (CROSS pages their texts and leaves at the end)):
# --start-node=<address>:<direction> starts before the riddle's tablet,
# facing it (src/main/Main.gd). CROSS opens it (the labyrinth runs it as its
# sub game mode, drawn in 2D over the floor: its top layer), shots while it
# fades in and after the cursor moved, then CROSS until the labyrinth is back,
# the fade out, a turn away and back.
wait 3000
shot r01_before
pos before
log before
tap CROSS
wait 300
shot r02_opening
wait 1200
shot r03_open
log open
tap RIGHT
wait 400
tap DOWN
wait 400
shot r04_moved
log moved
mark
tap CROSS
wait 700
shot r_#_leaving
loopuntil !sub=miniGames 12000
wait 1500
shot r05_left
log left
tap LEFT
wait 700
shot r06_away
tap RIGHT
wait 700
shot r07_back
log back
quit
