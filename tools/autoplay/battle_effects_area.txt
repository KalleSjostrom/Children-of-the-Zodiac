# Phase 4.4's rollout, part 5 (the card effects that are not particles): the
# effects in a battle area of its own (--load="Slot 1" --start=Serphia:
# BossBattleArea, all of its battle 3D in SubViewport chunks, and no light
# enabled when its frames end: Demolish's spheres, lit by that state, are
# almost black there). As effects_area.txt: god mode, CROSS pages the boss's
# words until the party's first turn; then the console casts Demolish 1, shot
# every frame until the animation ends (a_#), and at the next party turn Slow
# 1, every frame (b_#).
wait 4000
console god on
mark
tap CROSS
wait 500
loopuntil battlemode=1 30000
log turn1
console heal 100000 enemies
console cast Demolish:1
log cast1
mark
wait 16
shot a_#
loopuntil !battlemode=4 6000
log done1
mark
tap CROSS
wait 400
loopuntil battlemode=1 40000
log turn2
console heal 100000 enemies
console cast Slow:1
log cast2
mark
wait 16
shot b_#
loopuntil !battlemode=4 6000
log done2
wait 300
quit
