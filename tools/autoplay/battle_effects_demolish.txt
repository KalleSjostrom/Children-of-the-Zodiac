# Phase 4.4's rollout, part 5 (the card effects that are not particles):
# Demolish's lit spheres frame by frame, levels 1, 2 and 3 (one, two and three
# rings of four spheres; consecutive spheres share a batch up to 60,000
# vertices) on a floor (--load="Slot 1" --start="Berca Desert--South-mark0"
# --start-node=20:3: Ogre, Ogre; steps until the battle as battle_texts): the
# effect in the SubViewport after the legend's lines, lit by the light state
# the frame ends with. God mode; at each of three party turns the console
# heals the enemies and casts Demolish at the next level, shot every frame
