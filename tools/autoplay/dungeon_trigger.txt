# A trigger door's arrival (parity cases dungeon_trigger_*, phase 2 rollout):
# --load=Start --start="<floor>-mark0", where mark0 is a trigger door that
# has not been arrived at (TriggerDoor.arrive: after the fade-in its dialogs
# of maps/Labyrinth/Triggers/<trigger>.trig, its triggers, a tutorial offer).
# Shots while the floor fades in and the dialogs start, CROSS with a shot
# each until no dialog shows, then a turn, a step and the menu.
wait 1000
shot t01_fade_in
wait 2000
shot t02_arrived
log arrived
mark
tap CROSS
wait 900
shot t_#_dialog
loopuntil !dialog 30000
log talked
wait 1500
shot t03_after_dialogs
log after_dialogs
tap LEFT
wait 700
tap UP
wait 700
shot t04_walked
pos walked
tap TRIANGLE
wait 2500
shot t05_menu
log menu
tap CIRCLE
wait 2000
shot t06_after_menu
log after_menu
quit
