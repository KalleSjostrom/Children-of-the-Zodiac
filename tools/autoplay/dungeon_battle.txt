# A random battle on a floor, bounded in time (parity cases dungeon_battle_*,
# phase 2 rollout): --load="Slot 1" --start="<floor>-mark0"
# --start-node=<address>:<direction>, a cell with at least three free cells
# straight ahead (Labyrinth.checkEnterBattle wants two free cells ahead of
# the player). Steps forward and back (DOWN walks backwards, the facing
# stays) until a battle starts, shots of its start (the weather's front pass
# over it, the enemy colour), one round (cards with CROSS and RIGHT, TRIANGLE
# executes; CROSS pages its result screen, battlemode 5), then the console's
# "kill" at the next party turn, CROSS through the result screens, back on
# the floor.
wait 3000
shot b01_start
pos start
log start
mark
tap UP
wait 700
tap DOWN
wait 700
loopuntil sub=battle 90000
wait 300
shot b02_battle_start
wait 1500
shot b03_battle
log battle
mark
wait 200
loopuntil battlemode=1 8000
shot b04_party_turn
tap CROSS
wait 300
tap RIGHT
wait 300
tap CROSS
wait 300
shot b05_chosen
tap TRIANGLE
wait 1200
shot b06_exec
wait 1300
shot b07_turn
log round
mark
wait 200
loopuntil !battlemode=4 15000
mark
tap CROSS
wait 600
loopuntil !battlemode=5 15000
mark
wait 200
loopuntil battlemode=1 20000
console kill
wait 1000
shot b08_killed
log killed
mark
tap CROSS
wait 800
shot b_#_result
loopuntil !sub= 60000
wait 2000
shot b09_after
log after
pos after
tap LEFT
wait 700
shot b10_turned
quit
