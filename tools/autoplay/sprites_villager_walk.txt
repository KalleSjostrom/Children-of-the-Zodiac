# Every walking frame and two standing poses of a 56-high villager, for the
# character sprite sheets (docs/VILLAGER_SPRITES_PLAN.md; case
# sprites_villager_walk, --load="Slot 1" --start=Alares). A shot every 100 ms
# catches each 150 ms walk frame.
#
# Flog (Alares, image GTboygreen, 36x56) is on screen at the start: in the
# first 16.5 s of his patrol he walks down, right, up, stands facing right,
# walks down, left, up, right, stands facing up and walks left, so the shots
# show all 16 frames. The party (Kin, Celis, Zalzi) stands meanwhile.
wait 1500
mark
shot f#
wait 100
loopuntil nomatch 16500
pos end
quit
