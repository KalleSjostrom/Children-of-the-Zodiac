# A labyrinth boss (parity cases dungeon_boss_*, phase 2 rollout):
# --load="Slot 1" (the boss alive) --start="<floor>-mark0". The console's
# "boss" goes next to the boss, facing it (it enters the floor again at its
# save point and starts at that node, Load.labyrinthPosition); a step forward
# starts the boss battle, which runs inside the labyrinth's frame
# (Labyrinth.enterBattle: its sub game mode, drawn over the floor); CROSS
# pages the boss's dialog until the party's turn, the console's "kill" wins
# it, CROSS pages the result screens and the dialogs after it until the
# battle is over (and the story the boss starts, if any).
wait 3000
shot k01_start
console boss
wait 4000
shot k02_next_to_boss
pos next_to_boss
log next_to_boss
tap UP
wait 300
shot k03_stepping
wait 1200
shot k04_battle
log battle
mark
tap CROSS
wait 500
shot k_#_dialog
loopuntil battlemode=1 30000
shot k05_party_turn
log party_turn
console kill
wait 1000
shot k06_killed
wait 2000
shot k07_killed_later
log killed
mark
tap CROSS
wait 800
shot k_#_after
loopuntil !sub= 60000
wait 2000
shot k08_over
log over
wait 3000
shot k09_later
log later
quit
