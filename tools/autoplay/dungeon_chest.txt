# --start=<floor> --start-node=<address>:<direction> --no-encounters, the
# start node facing a closed chest: open it (its second frame and the
# dialog of what it held), then close the dialog. Used for chests whose
# content failed to load (Menthu's Lair--Hall of Memories, chest 165).
wait 3000
pos start
shot k01_facing_chest
log chest
tap CROSS
wait 300
shot k02_opened
wait 1000
shot k03_dialog
log dialog
tap CROSS
wait 800
shot k04_closed
log closed
quit
