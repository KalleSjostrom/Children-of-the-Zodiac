# Phase 4.4's rollout, part 5 (the card effects that are not particles): Death's
# and Slow's images frame by frame (--load="Slot 1" --start="Berca
# Desert--South-mark0" --start-node=20:3: Ogre, Ogre). Steps until the battle
# (as battle_texts); god mode. At the first party turn the console heals the
# enemies and casts Death 1 on an Ogre (ImageParticleSystem: the image fading
# in, its four flips between the two eyes' images, the hit, the fade to 0),
# shot every frame until the animation ends (d_#); at the next party turn
# Slow 1 (SlowParticleSystem: the clock and its hands turning, fading in and
# out), every frame (s_#).
wait 3000
console god on
mark
tap UP
wait 700
tap DOWN
wait 700
loopuntil sub=battle 90000
mark
wait 100
loopuntil battlemode=1 30000
log turn1

loopuntil !battlemode=4 6000

wait 100

wait 16
shot s_#
loopuntil !battlemode=4 6000
log slow_done
