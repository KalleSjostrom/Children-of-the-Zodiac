# Uses the object ahead, one script for every kind (parity cases dungeon_use_*,
# phase 2 rollout): --start-node=<address>:<direction> starts on the cell
# before the object, facing it (src/main/Main.gd). CROSS uses it (a chest
# opens, a lever or button plays its frames, a hidden door takes a push, a
# villager, bomb or mission chest starts talking), shots during its
# animation; then CROSS with a shot each until no dialog shows (dialogs page
# on and close; a lever shows its "already pulled" message once, as for a
# player who presses again), a look away and back, the menu (its thumbnail)
# and back.
wait 3000
shot u01_before
pos before
log before
tap CROSS
wait 150
shot u02_using
wait 550
shot u03_using_later
wait 1300
shot u04_used
log used
mark
tap CROSS
wait 900
shot u_#_dialog
loopuntil !dialog 15000
log talked
pos after
tap LEFT
wait 700
shot u05_away
tap RIGHT
wait 700
shot u06_back
log back
tap TRIANGLE
wait 2500
shot u07_menu
log menu
tap CIRCLE
wait 2000
shot u08_after_menu
log after_menu
quit
