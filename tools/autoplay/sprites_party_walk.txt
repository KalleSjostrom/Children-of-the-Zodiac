# Every walking frame of the party, for the character sprite sheets
# (docs/VILLAGER_SPRITES_PLAN.md; case sprites_party_walk, --load="Slot 1"
# --start=Berca): Kin and Celis (36x56) and Zalzi (36x55) walk up, right,
# left and down, 1.5 s each (down until the guard, before the town's edge),
# with a shot every 100 ms; the followers turn where the leader turned, so
# each one shows every direction's four frames. Then they stand.
wait 1500
guard 40
press UP
mark
shot u#
wait 100
loopuntil nomatch 1500
release UP
press RIGHT
mark
shot r#
wait 100
loopuntil nomatch 1500
release RIGHT
press LEFT
mark
shot l#
wait 100
loopuntil nomatch 1500
release LEFT
press DOWN
mark
shot d#
wait 100
loopuntil nomatch 1500
release DOWN
mark
shot s#
wait 100
loopuntil nomatch 1000
pos end
quit
