# --load="Slot 1" --start=landAlares --bridge-test: the bridge test
# (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md section 8; parity case bridge_test). The world map
# (legacy until phase 4.1) shows tools/bridge_test/BridgeTest.tscn (panel,
# grid, frame counter, Camera2D with an offset) instead of its background
# tiles; roads, nodes, the player, the top layer and the node name are legacy
# drawn on top. Since phase 1 every village has its own scene, so the test
# moved here from Alares.
#
# Fade-in: the legacy fade quad covers the scene.
wait 150
shot bt01_fadein
wait 200
shot bt02_fadein_later
wait 3150
shot bt03_start
log start
# Walk the road down to East Cross: the player and the road dots over the
# scene, not moved by its Camera2D.
tap DOWN
wait 600
shot bt04_walking
wait 2400
shot bt05_east_cross
log east_cross
# Open the menu: the world map is suspended, its scene leaves the tree but
# is kept (no world= in the log); the menu's thumbnail includes the scene.
tap TRIANGLE
wait 2500
shot bt06_menu
log in_menu
# Back: the scene is in the tree again, its frame counter continued.
mark
tap CIRCLE
wait 900
loopuntil mode=landscape 15000
wait 1500
shot bt07_back_from_menu
log back_from_menu
# Right to Grivera and into the labyrinth: the world map fades out over the
# scene, then the labyrinth shows and World is empty. The world map is still
# Organizer.previousGameMode, so its scene is not freed yet.
tap RIGHT
wait 3000
shot bt08_grivera
tap CROSS
wait 300
shot bt09_leaving
mark
wait 500
loopuntil mode=labyrinth 15000
wait 2000
shot bt10_labyrinth
log labyrinth
# Close the floor's message and open the menu in the labyrinth: the next
# mode switch drops the world map, which frees its scene ("BridgeTest: freed"
# in the output).
mark
tap CROSS
wait 500
tap TRIANGLE
wait 1000
loopuntil mode=menu 15000
wait 1500
shot bt11_labyrinth_menu
log labyrinth_menu
quit
