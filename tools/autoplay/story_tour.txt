# A cutscene from its start (--start=<story>), for the parity cases story_*
# (phase 1 rollout, tools/parity/cases.txt): a shot every 1.5 s and a CROSS
# every 0.5 s (it ends a dialog page that waits for input; it does nothing
# while actors move), until the game mode is no longer VillageStory or for
# at most 180 s. Stories that chain into the next one (Elder of Dellna Saves
# the Day -> Celis Wakes Up, ...) keep going until the time is up. The shots
# are numbered (s_002, s_003, ...); the log lines name the story, so a
# parity compare also checks the timing of every switch.
wait 100
shot s_00_start
mark
wait 250
tap CROSS
wait 500
shot s_#
wait 250
tap CROSS
wait 500
tap CROSS
log tick
loopuntil !VillageStory 180000
wait 1500
shot s_99_after
log end
quit
