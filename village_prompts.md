A 16-bit pixel-art town map from a classic fantasy RPG: a peaceful, leafy medieval
village in a forest clearing on a sunny day.

VIEW: elevated orthographic front view, roughly three-quarters overhead with no
perspective. Every building faces the viewer, with its front wall shown straight on
and its roof foreshortened above it. Do not use an isometric or diagonal angle, a
straight-down top view, or vanishing points. Use crisp, hand-placed pixels, a limited
palette, dark outlines and soft drop shadows falling to the right.

ARCHITECTURE: white plaster walls with dark-brown timber framing, steep red/coral roofs
of rounded fish-scale tiles, green-painted window frames, wooden plank doors and flower
boxes under the windows. Pale sandy dirt paths lead to every door; bright, lightly
textured grass covers everything else.

THE VILLAGE MUST CONTAIN ALL OF THESE (placement and size are free):

1. CHURCH, the most distinctive building. Grey stone-brick walls, a steep brown
   wooden-shingle roof and a central octagonal lantern tower with gold trim and gothic
   arched windows. A pale-blue dome with a cross on top crowns the tower. A tall
   stained-glass window sits above an arched, iron-banded wooden double door. Two
   statues of robed women stand in niches on either side of the door.

2. BLACKSMITH / WEAPON SHOP. A small white cottage with crossed swords mounted on the
   wall above the door and a rooster weathervane on the roof. An open-sided forge is
   attached to its side under an extension of the same roof. It has a cobblestone
   floor, a black iron furnace, a stone pillar with a red heraldic shield, stacked
   firewood and a stone chimney. Wooden barrels stand outside, iron helmets are
   displayed under the window, a wooden sign board shows a crossed-swords emblem, and
   a red mailbox stands by the door.

3. INN, the tallest building. Three stories of white plaster under a very steep red
   roof, with a rooster weathervane at the peak. A hanging wooden sign on the facade
   reads "INN". Every window has a box of red flowers, wooden tubs of flowers line the
   front, and the door is off to one side.

4. MAYOR'S HOUSE. A fine two-story half-timbered house with curved timber braces. A
   red heraldic banner with a gold crest hangs above the front door. It has window
   boxes of orange flowers, potted shrubs on both sides of the door and a wooden
   notice board in front. It is clearly nicer than an ordinary home.

5. LIBRARY, the widest building. A long, low house with two wings, a row of dormer
   windows in the roof and a central gabled entrance. A sign of an open book and a
   quill hangs above the door. Red flowerbeds run along the whole front, and a small
   tree grows in a half-barrel planter at one corner.

6. ITEM SHOP. A modest cottage with a small tiled porch roof over the door, stone
   steps and a little signpost by the steps. Next to it, inside a white picket fence,
   is an open-air market stall. A stout, mustached shopkeeper in a blue cap stands
   behind a wooden counter with potion bottles, surrounded by barrels, clay pots, a
   crate of bread loaves and flower planters.

7. SCHOLAR'S COTTAGE. A small, cozy half-timbered house with a steep red roof, a
   rooster weathervane, ivy climbing one corner and flowers by the door.
